One or two sentences on the setting: textless accent conversion in discrete unit space with real parallel supervision, over the bidirectional accent map (L2 → L1 removal and L1 → L2 installation).

One or two sentences on the finding: conversion splits into removal and installation, the two are asymmetric, and the asymmetry is a property of the substrate rather than of the model.

One sentence on what the samples below show, and one on the evaluation.

Audio samples

Every row is one utterance, and every system on that row converted the same recording of it. Source and Target are natural recordings of the same prompt by the two speakers — the target is what a perfect conversion would sound like, not a system output. Ours and the baselines follow.

The 33 utterances here (15 removal, 18 installation) are exactly the stimuli of the subjective listening test, chosen by a blind triage pass in which items were judged unlabelled and shuffled.
